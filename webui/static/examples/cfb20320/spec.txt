A solid cylindrical bushing, 100mm OD and 80mm long, with a concentric stepped bore on its axis. From the top end face, counterbore a 49mm diameter pocket to a depth of 25mm, leaving a flat annular shoulder 15mm above the mid-length. From the bottom end face, bore a 29mm diameter hole 55mm deep so that it breaks through into the floor of the 49mm counterbore, forming one continuous stepped passage the full 80mm length. Leave all edges sharp, with the outside diameter plain along its full length.
