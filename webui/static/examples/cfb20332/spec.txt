A 60mm diameter disc, 10mm thick, with an 18mm diameter blind bore on the central axis entering from the bottom face to a depth of 9.2mm, leaving a 0.8mm web below the top face. Break the bore's edge with a 45° chamfer at the 18mm bore diameter. Machine eight sector-shaped pockets into the top face, equally spaced around the bore, leaving eight radial spokes between the hub and the outer rim. Blend each pocket with 27mm radius internal fillets (eight places) and 14mm radius edge fillets (eight places). Pocket depth is not dimensioned here and should be set so that the finished solid volume is approximately 18,165 mm³.
