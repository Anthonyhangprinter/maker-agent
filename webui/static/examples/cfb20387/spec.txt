It should have a closed top at Z=+20 and four side walls, with every wall 4mm thick and the Z=-20 face left fully open onto a 52x52x36mm internal cavity. Drill a 13mm diameter hole 4mm deep through the top wall at its centre (30mm from both the X and Y edges), breaking into the cavity. In the +X side wall, drill two 9mm diameter holes 4mm deep through the wall, both at mid-height (Z=0) and spaced at Y=+15mm and Y=-15mm from centre (30mm apart). In the -Y side wall, drill one 7mm diameter hole 4mm deep through the wall, centred at X=0 and Z=0.
