A raised-face slip-on style flange: a 130mm OD disc, 14mm thick, with a concentric 90mm OD raised face standing 3.5mm proud of one side, for an overall height of 17.5mm. Bore a 65mm diameter hole on the centre axis, through the full 17.5mm height. Drill eight 11mm holes through the 14mm flange thickness on a 110mm bolt circle concentric with the bore, equally spaced at 45°. Place the first pair of holes on one in-plane axis at 10mm in from the outer edge, so the holes sit outside the raised face.
