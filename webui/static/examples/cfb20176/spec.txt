Please make a pillow-block saddle with an 84mm-long by 25mm-deep base flange, 5mm thick. Centre a 44mm-wide raised block on the flange's length, running the full 25mm depth and bringing the overall height to 27mm above the flange underside. Cut a semicircular cradle of 17mm radius (34mm diameter) into the top of the block, with its axis running along the 25mm depth at the top face and centred on the part's length. This puts the bottom of the cradle 10mm above the flange underside. Drill two 6mm holes 5mm deep up from the underside of the flange, so they pass fully through its 5mm thickness. Place them on the mid-depth centreline at 33mm either side of the part centre (9mm in from each end).
